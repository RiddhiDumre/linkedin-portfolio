# Brag Plan: F1 Pit Lane Telemetry & Speed Challenge

## What is this app?
An interactive Formula 1 Pit Lane Telemetry simulator where you execute high-pressure tyre changes against a live broadcast clock, complete with tyre compound selection, mechanical isometric breakdowns, and authentic Scuderia Ferrari team radio roasts.

## The angle
High-octane Formula 1 broadcast professionalism directly colliding with pure Ferrari pit wall comedy. Fast, ultra-slick telemetry and race timing on top, pure "We are checking... Box box box!" chaos underneath.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Crimson broadcast graphics flash onto the screen: "BOX BOX BOX! PIT STOP SEQUENCE INITIATED" as the live official timing clock begins ticking at 00.000s and telemetry alerts lock in.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: Compound Selection & Crew Staging (3-7s)**: Switching tyre rack compounds (SOFT / MEDIUM / HARD) with mechanical clicks, tyres arming for the swap.
- **Beat 2: The Rapid Tyre Execution (7-12s)**: Four wheels detaching in exploded isometric precision (FL, FR, RL, RR) with real-time wheel nut torque locked.
- **Beat 3: Official Duration & Radio Roast (12-16s)**: The broadcast results modal slams down with 1.98s STATIONARY DURATION followed by the legendary Ferrari radio roast: *"WE ARE CHECKING... STAY OUT! NO WAIT, BOX BOX BOX!"*

## Outro / punchline (16-19s)
"ELITE EXECUTION // NEXT PIT STOP →" with Scuderia Ferrari live telemetry badge and glowing F1 speed challenge branding.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: F1 car enters pit box, broadcast telemetry ribbon arms the timer.
2. Key action: Driver calls tyre compound (SOFT red dot active) and wheel gun targets lock on.
3. Result: Broadcast telemetry modal delivers official time and the Ferrari team radio roast.
